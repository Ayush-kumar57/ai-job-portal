HOW THE AI WORKS

From resume to match score in 4 steps

01
Upload

Seeker uploads a PDF or DOCX resume. We pull out the raw text.

02
Clean (NLP)

Lowercase, remove stopwords and symbols, lemmatise words, extract skills.

03
Vectorise (TF-IDF)

Resume and job text become number vectors. Rare skills get more weight.

04
Match (Cosine)

The angle between the two vectors gives a score from 0 to 100%.
